Go Girls* Game Night

In September 2026 I started the Go Girls* Game Night, a monthly Go evening for women* in Heidelberg that I host together with Maya. The idea: an easy way into the game, and more women* who love Go.

I built the event's website with Claude Code and look after it: in German and English, made for phones, with dates that work themselves out, links to Meetup and Google Calendar, and a short introduction to Go.

Go Girls* Timer

The Go Girls* led to my next project: a Go clock in the same cute, girly style. I wanted one to practise for a tournament, so I built it with Claude Code as my pair programmer, from idea to a first working version in one day. It sits on a phone next to the board, has Fischer and byo-yomi time, statistics with a chart of the time per move and a voice in English and German, and works offline. It runs in any browser and is now in testing as an Android app on Google Play.
